# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Neighbors, community members, bands, venues, collectives, and organizers discovering and participating in local communities. The reference quilt serves Lancaster; the software serves independently operated quilts everywhere.

## Product Purpose

Open-source, self-hostable community infrastructure for discovering patches and events and organizing together. Communities retain their data and can leave through Seamrip.

## Brand Personality

Neighborly, handmade, direct. Preserve the existing mobile site's textile vocabulary and visual identity.

## Anti-references

Advertising feeds, engagement mechanics, data harvesting, and centralized control of community access. The native app must not silently select Lancaster or require a directory's permission to connect to another quilt.

## Design Principles

- The exit is part of the product.
- Quilts are independently operated; choosing one is explicit.
- Preserve familiar content and workflows while using native platform navigation and controls.
- Treat the official app as one client of the public API.
- Keep community participation available on the web.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Carry forward the web theme's AA contrast intent. The native adaptation uses semantic text sizes, VoiceOver labels, system navigation, and layouts that accommodate larger text. Do not communicate meaning through color alone.
